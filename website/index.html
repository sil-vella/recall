Gameplay

  • Tap the draw pile to draw a card.
  • Select a card from your hand to play Clear and collect mode — excluding collection cards.
  • Play cards with the same rank as the last played card out of turn.
  • Queens let you peek at face-down cards from any player’s hand, including your own.
  • Jacks let you swap any two cards from any player’s hand, including your own Clear and collect mode — including collection cards.

Clear and collect mode

Collect cards from the discard pile when they match your collection rank. Collecting all four cards of your rank wins the game.

Final round

If you think you have the least points during your turn, you can call final round after you play your card. That triggers the final round — this was your final turn, so you won’t play again.

Winning

  • Player with no cards wins.
  • Player with least points wins.
  • If same points, player with least cards wins.
  • If same points and same cards, player who called final round wins.

Clear and collect mode — the player who collects all four cards of their rank wins the game.

Scoring snapshot

  • 2–10 — face value
  • Ace — 1  ·  Q / J / K — 10
  • Joker — 0
